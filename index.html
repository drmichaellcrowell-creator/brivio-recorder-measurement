The Live Conversation profile matches that recorder's candidate order, MediaRecorder options, unsupported-format refusal and its start() with no timeslice — but not its audio source. That recorder wraps a live room's already-published call track; this page uses a standalone microphone, which no page can substitute for the real thing. A size measured here is therefore indicative of that recorder's settings, not equivalent to the live-room track, and it does not replace a real classroom test.
